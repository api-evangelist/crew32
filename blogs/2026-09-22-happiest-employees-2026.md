The 10th annual list of Happiest Employees – part of Comparably’s Best Places to Work series – is based on millions of anonymous employee ratings across tens of thousands of large and SMB companies over the past year. The ranked companies are segmented by Large (more than 500 employees) and SMB (500 or fewer employees) organizations.
